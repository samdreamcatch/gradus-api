# Improve every move with AI

Add an **"Improve moves with AI"** button to the plan editor on Admin → Social plans. It works the same way whether you are creating a new plan or editing one you already made.

## How it works

1. Click **Improve moves with AI** (sparkle icon, next to the moves header).
2. A sheet slides up with a prompt box, pre-filled with a suggestion you can change: *"Make every move clearer and more coachable: tighten the how-to, sharpen the cue, and explain why it matters for this sport and level."*
3. Click **Improve**. AI rewrites every move's name, how-to, coaching cue, why it matters and target, following your prompt and keeping the same sport.
4. The improved moves replace the ones in the editor. Nothing is saved until you click **Save changes** or **Create**, and **Undo** puts the previous moves back.

Rules:
- The number and order of moves stay the same unless your prompt asks otherwise (max 12).
- No athlete names are added.
- If AI fails, your moves stay exactly as they were and an error message explains what happened.
