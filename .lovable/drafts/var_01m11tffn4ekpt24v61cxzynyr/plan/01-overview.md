# Competition recap prompt

When a parent or athlete follows skaters at a live competition, Gradus should ask them afterward how it went — and keep that reflection on the athlete's record where their coach can read it.

## What the person sees

1. **During the competition** — nothing new. Following a skater on a board, while signed in, quietly records "this account was at this competition."
2. **After the competition finishes** — the next time they open Gradus, a card appears at the top of their home screen: *"How did Fall Classic go?"* with **Write recap** and **Not now**. It reappears for up to 7 days, then stops asking.
3. **The recap sheet** — a bottom sheet in the app's paper style with four short prompts:
   - How did the competition go? (the same five-point scale used for practice sessions — Frustrated → Flowing)
   - What went well?
   - What didn't go so well?
   - Anything you'd like to share with your coach?
   Only the scale is required; the writing is optional. Submitting saves it and thanks them.
4. **Who it's about** — we assume it's their athlete in the sport the competition belongs to. The sheet only asks "Who is this about?" when the parent has more than one athlete in that sport; a name match against the followed skater still wins when we have one.

Parent and athlete each get their own prompt and their own recap, attributed by name — one does not close the other's.

## Where it lands

The recap is saved to the athlete's record only. No message is sent. It shows up:

- On the coach's athlete detail sheet, in the competition section next to results, labeled with who wrote it and when.
- To the parent/athlete themselves and any co-guardian on a Team Plan.

Nothing appears on the public board, and nobody outside the athlete's existing circle can see it.
