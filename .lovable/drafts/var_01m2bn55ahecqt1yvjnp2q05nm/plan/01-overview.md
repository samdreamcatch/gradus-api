# Getting-started checklist for new coaches

New coaches land on their roster with no idea what to do first. This adds a
three-step checklist that appears right after a coach finishes creating their
account, and that ticks itself off as they do each step.

## The three steps

1. **Upload or input your schedule** — opens the existing schedule import flow
   (photo, CSV, or manual entry).
2. **Invite parents or adult athletes** — opens the existing invite hub.
3. **Create your first practice plan** — goes to the plan builder.

Each step shows a state: done (struck through with a filled check), current
(expanded, with a short line of explanation and one button), or upcoming
(collapsed row). Only one step is expanded at a time — the first unfinished one,
exactly like the reference screenshot.
