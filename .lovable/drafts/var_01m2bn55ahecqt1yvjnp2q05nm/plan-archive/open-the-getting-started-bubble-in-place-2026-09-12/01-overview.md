# Open the getting-started bubble in place

Today the checklist lives in two places: a card near the top of the roster page, and a slide-up panel from the bottom of the screen on first visit. Neither uses the empty area you circled.

The change: the little chat button stays parked in the lower right, and tapping it opens the checklist as a thought bubble that grows upward from that button, floating over the empty space below the schedule. Tapping the X (or anywhere outside it) collapses it back into the button.

What stays the same:
- The three steps, their wording, and their auto-checking off as the coach does each thing.
- The green bubble look with the Gradus mark and the little tail pointing at the button.
- It disappears for good once all three steps are done.
