# Get-set-up checklist as a thought bubble

Turn the coach getting-started card into a small squared speech bubble in the
tennis-green pen colour, with the Gradus bar mark inside it. Closing it doesn't
make it disappear — it shrinks into a little bubble button in the corner that
reopens the checklist any time.

## What the coach sees

1. **First visit after signing up** — the checklist opens as it does today
   (a panel sliding up from the bottom of the screen), so the three steps are
   the first thing they read.
2. **On the roster page** — instead of today's plain row card, the checklist sits
   in a squared speech bubble: tennis-green tinted background, a small tail at
   the bottom-left, the Gradus mark in the corner of the bubble, the three steps
   inside, and a small close mark.
3. **Closed** — the bubble collapses with a quick shrink into a small round
   chat button pinned low on the right, showing the Gradus mark and how many
   steps are left. Tapping it opens the checklist back up.
4. **All three steps done** — the bubble and the little button both go away for
   good.
